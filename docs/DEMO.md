# SwitchForge demonstration

The completed synthetic workflow used a human, the real Mattermost conversation, Switch routing and the configured Codex agent. Four defective results blocked readiness. After separate human correction review, four corrected results passed and ReleaseProof reached READY_FOR_HUMAN_APPROVAL with zero blockers. The third human review recorded HUMAN_ATTESTED_FOR_DEMO. Its later effective state is EXPIRED.
